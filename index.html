    Threshold Configuration

    Status becomes Warning at or above the warning value and Critical at or above the critical value. Critical must be higher than Warning.

    Recovery uses a 3 % hysteresis band so a value hovering around a threshold does not flood you with alerts. Settings are saved in this browser.
